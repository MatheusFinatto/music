# Hub (homepage)

Scope: `src/app/[locale]/page.tsx` and the site shell. Mode: Operate (entry panel). Audience: any musician, first the owner and friends. Job: pick a tool and enter in one click. Content: tools from `src/tools/registry.ts` with honest status. Chosen by the owner after two re-rolls: the category standard (canon), sitting alongside Apple's app pages, with motion "more present".

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, generous launcher: the tools you can open today are the largest things on the page. It refuses novelty skins; the convention is the commitment.

OWN-WORLD: Soft grey ground, white rounded cards with one-step soft shadow, near-black ink, one brand blue for actions and "playing now", orange only for the target note. One sans family, tight display tracking, authored line icons in one stroke.

STORY: The visitor sees what the site is in one sentence, sees what works now, opens it; then sees what is coming, without dates.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Translucent sticky header (brand left, Laboratório right). Centered headline and one-line lead. Under it a wide animated staff: seven notes, a blue playhead sweeping, one orange target note. Then the first row of large tool cards, already visible at 900px height.

FORM: category standard (canon), chosen by the user from the safer round; seed:ce97be8b24d9fae4. Signature interaction: the staff playhead lighting each note as it passes; cards rise in sequence on load and lift on hover.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
